# Oregon Rent Increase Research (for rent-increase slider tooltip)

Researched 2026-07-12. Sources verified via full-page reads.

## Key data points (last ~5 years)

1. **Apartment List statewide median rent trajectory** (via PAROA Nov 2025 article, primary data: Apartment List Oct 2025 datasets):
   - Jan 2019: $1,246 | Jun 2021: $1,371 | Jun 2022: $1,525 (peak, +22% in ~18 months)
   - Jan 2023: $1,486 | Oct 2025: $1,462 (−4.1% off 2022 peak)
   - YoY Oct 2025: −1.3% statewide (first sustained negative since 2020)
   - 2021–2022 saw "explosive double-digit rent spikes"; 2023–2025 flat/declining.
   - Eugene outlier: +4.6% YoY (Oct 2025); Salem +1.1%; Portland metro −1.4%; Bend −3.1%.

2. **Oregon Employment Dept (QualityInfo, Apr 2025)**:
   - Median 1-BR rent in Oregon +14% ($154) from 2019 to 2023 (~3.3%/yr avg).
   - Avg 1-BR rent +20% ($209) 2017→2022, then −2% (−$24) 2022→2023.

3. **Oregon rent stabilization (SB 608, 2019; amended 2023)**:
   - Cap = 7% + CPI for buildings 15+ yrs old; 10% hard cap added in 2023 after the allowable max hit ~14.6% in 2023.
   - Max allowable by year: 2021: 9.2% | 2022: 9.9% | 2023: 14.6% | 2024: 10.0% | 2025: 10.0% | 2026: 9.5% (Oregon DAS; Capital Chronicle Sep 2025).
   - Actual market increases have run well below the caps since 2023.

## Simple 5-yr math (Apartment List statewide median)
- Jun 2021 $1,371 → Oct 2025 $1,462 ≈ +6.6% total ≈ ~1.5%/yr average over that window.
- But window start matters: 2020→2022 was ~+20%; averaging the last 5 full years
  (2020–2025) gives roughly 2–4%/yr depending on measure. A fair tooltip claim:
  "Oregon rents rose sharply in 2021–2022 (double-digit spikes), then cooled;
  averaged over the last 5 years, statewide increases work out to roughly 2–4%
  per year. Oregon law also caps most annual increases (9.5% max in 2026)."
