> **Status: draft for corporate compliance review.** These are content assets only. They are not scheduled, sent, uploaded, or published. Do not use a claim, disclosure, program reference, audience setting, email, or SMS message until Cornerstone corporate compliance and the account owner have approved it.

## Purpose and funnel rule

The campaign is designed to increase **completed mortgage-chat intakes**, not merely landing-page clicks or chat-launch clicks. Each paid placement should route to the existing landing page and make the first on-page action obvious: start the educational chat. A Roadmap request remains a valuable secondary path for visitors who prefer a written starting point.

The approved property-eligibility operating rule remains: use the active GeoSphere LMI + USDA RD classification after engagement to guide eligible-listing conversations. Do not use that rule to imply approval, individual eligibility, or prohibited audience targeting.

## 30-day nurture sequence — content only

The sequence is designed for leads who are eligible for the selected communication channel and have not opted out. It does **not** authorize sending messages. Any SMS use requires the existing consent language and corporate review; this library therefore treats email as the default draft channel.

| Timing / trigger | Audience | Draft subject | Draft purpose and core message | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 — Roadmap request | Roadmap lead | Your First-Time Homebuyer Roadmap | Deliver the requested Roadmap, thank the lead, and invite them to use the educational mortgage chat when they are ready to organize questions. | Start the chat |
| Day 2 — Cost-of-waiting used | Roadmap lead with cost-of-waiting scenario | A closer look at your timing questions | Refer to the lead’s own calculator inputs as an educational recap; explain that assumptions can change and invite a chat to discuss next questions. | Review timing in chat |
| Day 4 — 2-1 buydown used | Roadmap lead with buydown scenario | Questions to ask about payment structure | Refer to the lead’s own scenario as an example for discussion, not an offer; remind them program availability and qualification apply. | Discuss your questions |
| Day 7 — Completed chat | Completed chat intake | Your first-home planning recap | Thank the lead for completing the chat, recap their stated goals, and invite a short follow-up conversation. | Choose your next step |
| Day 10 — Homes-list interest | Completed chat with homes-list request | Let’s narrow the home search questions | Explain that a list can be better when it is tied to a city/county, timing, budget, and eligibility discussion; offer to review search criteria. | Refine your search |
| Day 14 — Buyer-agent request | Completed chat with buyer-agent request | A local buyer-agent connection when you are ready | Offer to connect the lead with a local buyer’s agent for showings or search planning, subject to the lead’s stated preference. | Request a connection |

| Day 30 — No response | Eligible non-responsive lead | Your next first-home question | Invite the lead to reply with the one question they want to solve next. Do not imply urgency, special availability, or approval. | Start with one question |

### Scenario-aware message rules

Use a lead’s calculator data only to recap the exact inputs they provided. Do not reframe calculated outcomes as a promise or personalized recommendation. For a cost-of-waiting message, explain that rent, appreciation, rate, price, and payment assumptions can change. For a 2-1 buydown message, state that seller concessions, rates, underwriting, eligibility, and program availability must be verified case by case.

## 30-day cadence safety checks

| Check | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Email preferences | Send only through the lender-approved system and honor unsubscribe or suppression rules. |
| SMS consent | Do not text unless the lead has valid, approved consent and the SMS copy is compliance-approved. |
| Duplicate sources | Suppress duplicate messages when a lead completes a chat after already receiving the Roadmap. Use the completed-chat recap as the next meaningful message. |
| Human handoff | Stop automated nurture drafts when the lead books, is actively working with a loan officer, or requests agent help; move to the documented human follow-up process. |
| Claims | Use approved language only. Keep any disclosure and program discussion required by compliance. |
