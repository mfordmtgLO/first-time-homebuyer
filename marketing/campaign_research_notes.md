# Mortgage Buyer-Attraction Campaign — Official Research Notes

## Regulatory and program references

| Source | Campaign implication |
|---|---|
| CFPB Regulation Z, 12 CFR §1026.24: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/24 | Specific credit terms must be actually available. Required disclosures must be clear and conspicuous. Mortgage rate/payment/down-payment/finance-charge claims can trigger additional disclosures. Reduced-rate/buydown claims require limited-term and later-rate context as applicable. |
| Oregon Division of Financial Regulation, Mortgage Industry: https://dfr.oregon.gov/business/licensing/financial/mortgage/pages/index.aspx | Oregon states that mortgage advertising is governed by federal and state rules. It identifies two common Truth in Lending problems: note-rate advertising without APR and trigger terms such as down-payment amounts without required disclosures. |
| Oregon Housing and Community Services, Flex Lending: https://www.oregon.gov/ohcs/homeownership/homebuyers/pages/flex-lending.aspx | Flex Lending is offered through approved lenders statewide and may pair with DPA. DPA may be used up to 100% of the borrower cash requirement to close, subject to program rules. FirstHome and NextStep are the two listed products. Avoid representing eligibility or payment outcomes as guaranteed. |

## Meta policy reference

| Source | Campaign implication |
|---|---|
| Meta Business Help, audiences for housing, employment, or financial products/services: https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2220749868045706 | For U.S. financial-products-and-services campaigns, Meta requires the applicable Special Ad Category. ## Google policy and local-placement references

| Source | Campaign implication |
|---|---|
| Google Ads, Restricted targeting in Personalized advertising: https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/143465?hl=en | U.S./Canada housing and consumer-finance campaigns are access-to-opportunities categories. Do not use age, gender, parental status, marital status, or ZIP targeting. City and radius targeting are allowed. ## Current landing-page conversion inventory

The public landing page at https://firsttimehomebuyer.manus.space currently supports multiple buyer-intent actions: a Roadmap form with name, email, phone, and SMS consent; a mortgage-intake chat; cost-of-waiting and 2-1 buydown educational calculators; call, text, email, Calendly, and secure application links; and a first-time buyer program explanation. The campaign should direct each ad family to the matching on-page section and distinguish educational estimates from credit offers or quotes.
