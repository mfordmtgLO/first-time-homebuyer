## Compliance guardrails before creative review

Mortgage and consumer-credit advertisements require careful approval. Avoid unsubstantiated savings, guaranteed approvals, fixed payments, rate promises, or program eligibility claims. If an ad states specific credit terms, additional Truth in Lending advertising rules can apply. [1]

For U.S. Meta campaigns, classify eligible campaigns under the applicable **Financial Products and Services** special category and keep audience settings within the platform’s restrictions. [2] For Google campaigns concerning consumer finance or housing, use the platform’s restricted “access to opportunities” policy framework and do not use prohibited demographic or ZIP-code targeting in the U.S. or Canada. [3]

All final ads should retain the approved Equal Housing Opportunity, NMLS, company, and any required state disclosure treatments prescribed by the lender’s compliance team. The ad manager should use approved source documents, not assumptions, for every license, company, rate, payment, down-payment, program, and availability statement.
