## Cover

# Mike Ford Admin Team Management Framework

## Secure downstream loan-officer onboarding, oversight, and growth operations

Mike Ford · Cornerstone First Mortgage

## Slide 1

# One controlled operating system, many accountable loan officers

- Mike Ford remains the administrator, manager, and compliance-review point for the first-time-homebuyer platform.
- Each downstream loan officer works from an independently authenticated, role-scoped dashboard workspace.
- The model supports local lead conversion and agent-partner growth without mixing another officer’s records or advertising activity.

## Slide 2

# Clear roles keep access intentional

| Role | What the role controls |
|---|---|
| **Mike Ford Admin** | Creates profiles, assigns access, prepares workspaces, approves templates, monitors activity, and can suspend or review any team workspace. |
| **Downstream loan officer** | Works assigned leads, chats, campaigns, quotes, listings, partner activity, and approved templates inside their own workspace. |
| **System** | Enforces authenticated access, data scoping, handoff readiness, and read-only controlled sharing. |

## Slide 3

# Provision before access—not after

- Mike starts by selecting a saved loan-officer profile and entering that officer’s exact work email.
- The new workspace begins as a **setup draft**; the officer cannot activate it yet.
- Mike records readiness for approved templates, GeoSphere listing resources, and Oregon agent-source workflow before release.
- A clear **Release secure handoff** action makes readiness a deliberate management decision.

## Slide 4

# Secure handoff protects the launch moment

1. Mike creates the officer’s saved profile and assigns the sign-in email.
2. Mike prepares the workspace and confirms selected shared resources are ready.
3. Mike adds an optional handoff note and releases the workspace.
4. The officer signs in with the assigned email and activates the released workspace.

> The application never issues or stores a dashboard password, temporary password, or reset token.

## Slide 5

# Each officer gets a scoped working dashboard

- **Lead flow:** assigned Roadmap leads, qualified Mortgage Chats, and platform leads stay attributed to the officer’s workspace.
- **Planning tools:** saved payment quotes, campaign spend, and private LO+Agent spend-tracker records stay account-scoped.
- **Co-brand growth:** property-page inventory, paired-agent profile, and approved partner workflows remain connected to the assigned loan-officer profile.
- **Separation:** a downstream officer cannot browse Mike Ford’s or a teammate’s private dashboard data.

## Slide 6

# Shared sources remain centrally governed

| Resource | Operating approach |
|---|---|
| **GeoSphere listing inventory** | Mike controls the authorized source connection and confirms the downstream workspace is prepared to use approved listing inventory. |
| **Oregon agent source** | Mike manages the authorized source, sync quality, and partner-creation governance before downstream use. |
| **Loan-officer advertising accounts** | Future officer-specific connections can attach to the officer’s own workspace while Mike retains management visibility. |

## Slide 7

# Template governance prevents uncontrolled copy

- Mike’s email, text, and outreach templates are **private by default**.
- Mike can make an approved template **Team read-only** for controlled copying without source edits.
- Downstream officers can submit a proposed email or text template for review.
- Mike can approve it for the team, approve it privately, or reject it with feedback; unapproved drafts are not available as team templates.

## Slide 8

# Shadow review creates practical management visibility

- Mike can open an administrator-only snapshot of any active team workspace.
- The review displays downstream Roadmap, chat, platform-lead, saved-quote, and spend activity without auto-messaging or changing records.
- A private team activity feed refreshes with newly captured leads and saved quote scenarios.
- Performance comparison highlights captured leads, progressed leads, conversion rate, and saved quote count across active officers.

## Slide 9

# Conversion reporting supports coaching—not lending decisions

- The dashboard compares officer workflow progress using captured leads and documented progression statuses.
- Booked Roadmap leads and qualified platform leads contribute to the internal conversion measure.
- Metrics help Mike identify follow-up rhythm, template needs, campaign quality, and agent-partner opportunities.
- Reporting is an operational management aid—not a credit, eligibility, compensation, or compliance determination.

## Slide 10

# Paid-ad account connection is ready for the next phase

- The current framework already isolates each officer’s platform-lead inbox, campaign spend, saved quotes, and performance history.
- A future connection screen can allow each officer to authorize only their own Meta Ads or Google Ads account.
- Imported lead forms can be mapped to the officer’s workspace and tracked with their own campaigns and source attribution.
- Activation remains gated by advertising-account credentials, form mappings, consent language, and corporate compliance approval.

## Slide 11

# Mike’s repeatable onboarding checklist

1. Complete the loan-officer profile and public/co-branded branding details.
2. Assign the exact work email and create the pending workspace.
3. Prepare approved templates and confirm source readiness for listings and Oregon agent workflow.
4. Review the setup, add handoff instructions, and release secure handoff.
5. Confirm first activation, monitor early lead flow, and use shadow review for coaching and compliance oversight.
